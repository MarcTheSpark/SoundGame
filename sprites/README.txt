Lost & Sound sprites, exported from the design canvas.

1x/   one pixel per art pixel (use these in an engine, scale with nearest-neighbour)
8x/   scaled up 8x with hard edges, for mockups
spritesheet.png + spritesheet.json   all 1x sprites packed in one image, with x/y/w/h per sprite

Note: on the canvas some sprites were drawn with slightly non-square pixels (7x8 or 10x12 px);
these exports use square pixels, so they look a touch narrower or wider than the mockups.
Not included: fireworks, confetti, sound-wave bars and drips, which are generated by code, not drawn.
